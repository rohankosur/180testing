005 / Courtside Math

problem of the week
Challenge #42 · AMC 12 / AIME Level 84 Students Solved · Current Week

Find the remainder when \( 2^{2026} + 3^{2026} \) is divided by \( 13 \).

A test of modular arithmetic intuition. Notice that 13 is prime, so Fermat’s Little Theorem guarantees that \( a^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13} \) for any integer \( a \) coprime to 13.

Step-by-Step First Principles Derivation:

1. By Fermat's Little Theorem: \( a^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13} \).

2. Divide the exponent \( 2026 \) by \( 12 \): \( 2026 = 12 \times 168 + 10 \). Hence, \( 2026 \equiv 10 \pmod{12} \).

3. This reduces our expression to: \( 2^{10} + 3^{10} \pmod{13} \).

4. Powers of 2: \( 2^4 = 16 \equiv 3 \). Then \( 2^8 \equiv 9 \equiv -4 \). Thus, \( 2^{10} = 2^8 \times 4 \equiv (-4) \times 4 = -16 \equiv 10 \pmod{13} \).

5. Powers of 3: \( 3^3 = 27 \equiv 1 \). Then \( 3^{10} = (3^3)^3 \times 3 \equiv 1^3 \times 3 \equiv 3 \pmod{13} \).

6. Sum the residues: \( 10 + 3 = 13 \equiv 0 \pmod{13} \).

Result: The remainder is precisely 0.

Where are you located for in-person lessons? ▼

Our institute studio is located at a private residence near South Windsor High School: 17 Longmeadow Drive, South Windsor, CT 06074. For families outside central Connecticut, we conduct full interactive live online cohorts.

What subjects and grades are taught at 180 Math? ▼

We offer structured math instruction from Grade 3 through high school, including Pre-Algebra, Algebra I & II, Geometry, Pre-Calculus, AP Calculus AB & BC, and AP Statistics. We also specialize in Digital SAT/ACT math strategy and AMC 8/10/12 competition math.

What is the cohort size? ▼

Cohorts are strictly kept to small, single-digit groups (typically 4–8 students). This intimate setting guarantees that every learner receives personal feedback and active attention, preventing any student from falling through the cracks.

Do you assign and help with school homework? ▼

Yes! Challenging school questions are always welcomed and analyzed during sessions. Additionally, we assign weekly targeted practice via textbook exercises and our DeltaMath portal, emphasizing thoughtful concept mastery over high-volume busywork.

When are fees due, and are they prorated? ▼

Fees are due by the 5th of each month. June fees are prorated based on the official school district closing date. For student absences, comprehensive whiteboard notes and instructional video summaries are shared, plus 1-on-1 check-in time during the following session.

Why does the curriculum include 36 weeks of lessons? ▼

The academic calendar spans 42 weeks. After accounting for 4 major holiday breaks (Thanksgiving, Christmas/Winter, Spring break) and 2 emergency teacher-cancellation weeks, students receive exactly 36 weeks of intensive 2-hour instruction.
